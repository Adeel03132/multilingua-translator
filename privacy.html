Privacy Policy

Multilingua – Translator for Kids · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Multilingua is made for children and families. We do not ask for names, emails or accounts, we show no ads, and we use no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Learning progress and settings are saved only on your device (browser local storage). We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

What is sent to other services

  • Online translation: if a word is not in the built-in dictionary, the word typed is sent to MyMemory (translated.net) or Lingva translation servers to get a translation. Nothing else about the child is sent.
  • Online voice: if the device has no voice for a language, the word is sent to Google's text-to-speech service to play its sound.
  • Microphone: if the microphone button is used, speech is processed by the device's or Google's speech recognition service to turn it into text. The microphone is used only while the button is on.
  • Fonts: text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

These services receive your device's internet (IP) address as part of any connection. We do not control them; see their own privacy policies. A parent can switch online translation off and set voice to "Device only" in the parent settings, so the app works offline with the built-in dictionary.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not sell or share data for advertising.
